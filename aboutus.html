The Recipe for Crumbn' Pastries

Ingredients

  • 1 small kitchen
  • 2 cups of stubborn dreaming
  • A generous pinch of butter
  • Every neighbor who taste-tested

Method

  1. Bake for friends and family.
  2. Hear "you should sell these" about 400 times.
  3. Open the doors. Bake fresh every morning.
  4. Repeat, happily, forever.

Yield: one very happy neighborhood

A day at Crumbn'

  1. 4:00 AM

    Dough time

    The kitchen is quiet, the mixer is not.

  2. 6:00 AM

    First tray

    Ovens on, and the whole street starts to smell amazing.

  3. 7:00 AM

    Doors open

    Still-warm cookies hit the counter.

  4. 12:00 PM

    Second batch

    A fresh round for the lunch crowd.

  5. 6:00 PM

    Last tray

    Whatever's left goes home with someone lucky.
