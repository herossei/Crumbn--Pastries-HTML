About Crumbn’ Pastries

Crumbn’ Pastries started with one oven, one well-loved cookie recipe and a big love for cozy mornings. Today we’re still a small bakery, still baking in small batches and still adding a little extra care to everything we make.

We believe a pastry should feel like a warm hug. That’s why we bake with:

  • Real butter and farm-fresh eggs
  • Pure vanilla and good chocolate
  • Simple, classic recipes made with love
